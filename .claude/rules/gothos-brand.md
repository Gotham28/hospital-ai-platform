# Gothos Labs brand

This project belongs to Gothos Labs. The company brand kit is in `brand/gothos/`.

Read `brand/gothos/BRAND.md` before you design, write or change anything people see:
screens, pages, emails, documents, slides.

- Follow its names and spelling (Gothos Labs, ELogBook, AROGYA, Mochi) and its way of
  writing, everywhere, now.
- Use the real owl logo file. Never redraw, recolour or restyle the owl or Mochi.
- Anything that speaks as the company (about pages, "by GOTHOS LABS" lines, company
  documents) uses the brand's colours, Poppins font and shapes.
- Do not restyle existing screens to match the brand as a side effect of other work.
  Restyling a product is its own task, scoped and approved by the developer.
- This project's own rules (`.agents/AGENTS.md`, the approved current task) always win.
  If they and the brand disagree, stop and ask the developer.
- The master copy is the "Gothos Labs" design system in Claude. If this kit and the master
  differ, the master wins; tell the developer.

## In this project (AROGYA)

- AROGYA has its own look today: Inter, and the `brand-*` and `iris-*` colours in
  `frontend/src/index.css`. Keep that look consistent in the screens that use it.
- Hospital and institution branding stays configurable. Never hard-code Gothos colours
  into a hospital's theme.
- Malayalam text uses Noto Sans Malayalam. Poppins has no Malayalam letters.
- Safety and privacy rules outrank brand. A health disclaimer stays as clear as those
  rules require.
