# Benchmark Release Licence Options

The project lead must select one of the following three open licences for the public benchmark release:

---

## Option 1: CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International) — *Recommended*

- **Plain Language:** Anyone may share, copy, adapt, and build upon the benchmark for any purpose, including commercial research, provided they cite the AROGYA paper.
- **Pros:** Maximum academic visibility, widely accepted by ACL/EMNLP and Hugging Face datasets.
- **Cons:** Commercial competitors can use the benchmark without sharing back their evaluations.

---

## Option 2: CC BY-SA 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0)

- **Plain Language:** Anyone may use and adapt the benchmark, but any modified versions or derived benchmarks must be released under the exact same licence terms.
- **Pros:** Guarantees community contributions and dataset extensions remain openly available.
- **Cons:** Some commercial and industry research labs avoid ShareAlike datasets due to legal conservatism.

---

## Option 3: CC BY-NC 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0)

- **Plain Language:** Non-commercial academic research only. Commercial entities cannot use the benchmark without explicit private licensing from the project owners.
- **Pros:** Prevents commercial entities from benchmarking or fine-tuning models on the dataset without compensation/agreement.
- **Cons:** Significantly reduces citations, excludes open-source models trained by industry labs (e.g., Meta, Google).
